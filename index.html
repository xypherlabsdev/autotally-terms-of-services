4. What the app is — and what it is not

AutoTally records and organises spending. It reads the transaction alerts your bank, card and payment apps post to your phone, parses them on your device, and turns them into a ledger you can categorise, budget against and export.

AutoTally is not a bank, a payment service or a financial institution. It cannot make payments, transfer money, cancel a charge, stop a mandate or contact your bank on your behalf. It has no connection to your bank account and never asks for banking credentials. Anything shown in the app is a record of what you or auto-capture entered — it is not a bank statement and has no effect on your actual accounts.

5. Auto-capture is an assistant, not an authority

Auto-capture is automated pattern matching over notification text. It works well for many banks and formats, and imperfectly for others. Transactions may be:

  • missed — if your bank sends no alert, sends it in a language or format the app does not read, or the system stops the app in the background;
  • duplicated — where a bank and a payment app both announce the same charge;
  • misread — wrong amount, wrong merchant, wrong direction or wrong category;
  • captured in error — from a message that resembles a transaction alert but is not one.

Captures the app is not confident about are held in a review queue for you to confirm or discard, and do not affect balances until you do. Even so, you are responsible for checking what the app records. Always treat your bank or card statement as the source of truth, and never rely on AutoTally for reconciliation, tax filing or any purpose where accuracy is critical.

Auto-capture also begins only when you enable it. It cannot see transactions or messages from before that point.
